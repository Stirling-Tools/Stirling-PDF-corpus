\section*{A Short Survey of Text Extraction}
Early work on document analysis focused on scanned pages \cite{nagy2000}. Later studies examined
born-digital files, where the text is stored with its fonts \cite{smith2003,jones1999}. Recent
surveys compare several extraction tools on large collections \cite{lee2005}.
\begin{thebibliography}{9}
\bibitem{jones1999} R.~Jones and P.~Brown. Reading characters from page description languages.
\emph{Journal of Document Engineering}, 12(3):145--167, 1999.
\bibitem{lee2005} K.~Lee, M.~Garc\'{\i}a, and T.~M\"uller. A comparison of extraction tools.
In \emph{Proceedings of the International Conference on Document Analysis}, pages 1001--1010, 2005.
\bibitem{nagy2000} G.~Nagy. Twenty years of document image analysis.
\emph{IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis}, 22(1):38--62, 2000.
