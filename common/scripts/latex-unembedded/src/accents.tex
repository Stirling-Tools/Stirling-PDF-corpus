\section*{Accented Words}
The caf\'e in Z\"urich served cr\`eme br\^ul\'ee and a na\"ive pi\~na colada.
Fran\c{c}ois met Ren\'ee and Bj\"orn near the \AA{}lesund harbour, then drove to S\~ao Paulo.
In German one writes Stra\ss{}e, Gr\"o\ss{}e, M\"adchen and \"Uberraschung.
Polish and Czech add \L{}\'od\'z, \v{C}esk\'y Krumlov and Dvo\v{r}\'ak.
Nordic words include \O{}resund, \ae{}ble, \oe{}uvre and \AE{}sir.
Spanish has \'el, se\~nor, a\~no and ma\~nana; Portuguese has cora\c{c}\~ao.
